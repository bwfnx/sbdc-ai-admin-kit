# SBA Metric → Milestone Mapping Reference

Maps an SBA metric (from the official definitions) to the corresponding Neoserra milestone on this skill's milestone list, with the rule for classifying it. Use it when deciding which milestone a client accomplishment maps to.

| SBA Metric (from definitions) | Corresponding Milestone | Notes / Classification Rule |
|---|---|---|
| New Business Start | Business Established / Business Start Impact | If the client formally starts operations with SBA Resource Partner support and there is clear mention of SBA's role → **Business Start Impact**. Otherwise → **Business Established**. |
| Capital Infusion (Transaction) | Other / Bought Business / Business Expansion / Change in Sales | Classified as a Capital Infusion milestone. Dollar amount required. If tied to an acquisition → **Bought Business**. If tied to expansion → **Business Expansion**. If tied to general growth → mark as **Change in Sales** with an infusion note. |
| Capital Infusion (Dollar Amount) | Other (Capital Infusion Recorded) | Always log under Capital Infusion with the value. |
| Jobs Supported / Jobs Created | Change in Full-Time Staff / Change in Part-Time Staff | Use increases/decreases in reported jobs. If full-time vs. part-time is unspecified → assume **Full-Time**. |
| Jobs Retained | Temporarily Closed Business / Temporarily Altered Business | If jobs were at risk but saved, log under a Staff Change. If the narrative links to a disruption (closure/alteration), note accordingly. |
| Revenue Growth | Change in Sales | When revenue is reported to have increased. Include dollar/percentage increase if available. |
| Profit Change (implied from revenue/expenses) | Change in Profits | Only record if profit (not just sales) is explicitly mentioned. |
| Unique Clients Served in Underserved Communities | Other (context only) | Not a milestone, but can support classification if tied to certifications/markets. |
| Entered New Foreign Markets (on the milestone list, not an SBA definition) | Entered New Foreign Markets | Direct mapping; triggered when export-market entry is noted. |
| Certifications (8(a), WOSB, EDWOSB, MBE, DBE, etc.) | Direct mapping to the matching milestone (e.g., 8(A) Certification Obtained) | When SBA-recognized certifications are achieved. |
| Change in Legal Form (not an SBA metric, but on the milestone list) | Changed Legal Form | Use if explicitly mentioned. |
| Closure (Temporary / Permanent / Reopen) | Temporarily Closed Business / Permanently Closed Business / Reopened Business | Use language cues from the client file. |
| Strategic Growth Plan Success | Strategic Growth Plan Success | Use when plan milestones are reported achieved. |
| Testimonials / Success Stories | Success Story | Also logged in the separate "Testimonials Table." |
